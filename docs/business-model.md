# Business Model: Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Record-Keeping and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-6330`
- ISCO-08: `6330`
- Occupation: Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmers
- Social impact: food-security, animal-welfare-integrity,
  household-livelihood-continuity, farm-record-keeping-transparency

## Customer

- subsistence-mixed-crop-and-livestock-farming households / holdings
- independent farmers/holders

## Offer

- planting/harvest/feeding-schedule/animal-condition-check-in record
  logging
- household labor/task farm-operation scheduling
- seeds/feed/tools procurement coordination
- always-escalating livelihood-concern flagging channel

## Revenue

- monthly holding retainer
- per-record logging fee

## Trust Controls

- farmer/holding record must be independently verified and registered
  before any action
- no proposal, anywhere in the closed op allowlist, can finalize a
  planting/harvest-timing agronomic decision or an animal-treatment/
  welfare/breeding decision, or override the farmer's own judgment
  about their household's crops or livestock — these are structurally
  absent, not merely gated
- any crop-failure, animal-welfare, injury-risk, or food-security
  concern surfaces ONLY via `:flag-livelihood-concern`, which always
  escalates to a human
- seeds/feed/tools orders above the registered per-holding cost
  ceiling always require human sign-off
- work, scheduling and supply records are auditable, not editable
